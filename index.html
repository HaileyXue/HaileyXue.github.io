GHAMUT · APPLIED AI

Turning documents into decisions

Document retrieval, summarization, and LLM-backed workflows that support multi-step appeal drafting.

  • Python
  • RAG
  • LLMs
  • Kafka
Explore the work +

The challenge

Make large volumes of unstructured documents searchable and useful in decision-support workflows.

My contribution

Built document processing pipelines, searchable structured databases, and backend APIs for Q&A, record summaries, and branched appeal drafting.

The impact

The workflow improved successful case outcomes from 46% to 73%. Separately, analysis of approximately 156,000 cases across 29 sites identified opportunities to automate approximately 47% of manual labor burden and 42% of labor cost. These are estimated opportunities, not realized savings.
